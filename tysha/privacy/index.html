Privacy.

What we keep, why, and for how long. Short, like everything we write.

Updated 2 November 2026No cookies · no analytics0 trackers
01 · Seven short answers0 trackers
01

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, a phone or an email, the region you have in mind and whether you already have a plot. Nothing else. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels.

02

Why

To answer your request and, if you want, to plan a silence audit of your plot. We never use it for advertising and never sell it.

03

Who sees it

The two partners of the bureau and, once a project starts, the acoustic engineer who works on it. Messages are stored by our email provider inside the EU.

04

How long

Twelve months after our last conversation. If we build together, as long as the contract and the law require.

05

Your rights

Ask what we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted: write to hello@tysha.studio and we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

06

This browser

Three small notes, kept in your browser and never sent anywhere. In local storage: that you closed the “No trackers” message. In session storage, forgotten when you close the tab: that you have already seen the opening animation, and whether you switched the sound on.

07

Who we are

TYSHA Architecture, Lviv — Warsaw. hello@tysha.studio. TYSHA is a concept project.
