Three houses, one family, three silences.

Pine slope above a lake · 2025
3 × 96 m² · A family compound
24 dB inside, on average20 min to Lviv40 m between the houses02 / 02 cases
01 · The briefThree households · one slope

One family, three houses, and nobody hears anybody.

The family
Parents, two grown children, guests

Andriy and Iryna wanted their children close, but not under one roof. Three small houses instead of one large one: each with its own door, its own view and its own silence.

02 · The slope31 dB on the path

We placed the houses by ear.

Three days with sound meters on the slope. The houses step down it, each turned a little, so no window looks into another and every bedroom faces the lake.

40mBetween the houses
31dBOn the path, by day
24dBInside, on average
03 · The houses96 m² each · two floors

Small, tall,
turned away.

Each house is a travertine cube with a larch wing. Downstairs, one double-height room and a window as tall as the pines; upstairs, the bedroom, under the roof and away from the path.

04 · MaterialsStone, larch, dry walls

What the slope already had.

Travertine for the walls, larch for the wings and the ceilings, and dry-stone walls from the plot to hold the terraces. Gravel on the paths: you hear someone coming, softly.
