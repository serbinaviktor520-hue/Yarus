01 · The briefBedroom · 41 → 21 dB

They asked for one thing: to sleep without earplugs.

The family
Olena, Mark, two kids

A family of four, a fourth-floor flat above a tram line, eleven years of 41 dB nights. They wanted forest, quiet and a half-hour drive to school and work.

02 · The plot24 dB at night

We listened for seven days before we said yes.

Four plots were on the table. Two had better views. Only this one stayed under 25 dB at night.

03 · The formOne long bar · 60 cm walls

Turned away
from the road.

A long, low travertine house on the shore. On the forest side, a walled courtyard with a single pine; between the two stone blocks, a glass hall opens to the lake by wide steps. To the road it shows rammed earth, 60 cm thick. To the lake, it opens completely.

04 · The planRoom by room · 38 → 21 dB
